✅ GREEN ZONE: Doing Well

  • Breathing normal
  • Usual cough/sputum
Actions:
  • Take preventer medication
  • Stay active
  • Avoid triggers
😊

⚠ YELLOW ZONE: Symptoms Worsening

  • Increased breathlessness
  • More cough/sputum
  • Sputum color change
Actions:
  • Start rescue medications
  • Use reliever more frequently
  • Contact clinic if no improvement
😐

🚨 RED ZONE: Severe Symptoms

  • Severe breathlessness
  • Can't speak in sentences
  • Lips/nails blue
Actions:
  • CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY
  • Use reliever medication
  • Take rescue medications
😞
