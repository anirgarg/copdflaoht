Preventative measures (for all COPD patients)

  • Vaccinations: pneumococcal vaccine, yearly influenza vaccine, yearly COVID vaccine, herpes zoster vaccine
  • Smoking cessation: counselling, nicotine replacement (STOP program), bupropion, varenicline (Resources)
  • Advanced care planning: discuss and document goals of care and wishes in the event of an exacerbation

COPD action plan

  • For increased dyspnea, cough: prednisone 40 mg PO daily x 5 days
  • If fever or green/brown sputum: add antibiotics (examples below)

Antibiotic examples

  • Amoxicillin-clavulanate 875 mg BID x 5–7 days
  • Levofloxacin 750 mg PO daily x 5–7 days
  • Moxifloxacin 400 mg PO daily x 5–7 days

Stable COPD management (pharmacological)

If FEV1 ≥80% predicted, few symptoms and no previous AECOPD

Prescribe either:

  • LABA (formoterol, salmeterol, indacaterol) or
  • LAMA (tiotropium, aclidinium, umeclidinium, glycopyrrolate)

If FEV1 <80% predicted, stable COPD without exacerbations, but significant symptom burden

Prescribe LABA + LAMA (or a combination product with both ingredients).

Inhaler examples — LABA

  • Salmeterol Diskus 50 mcg: 1 puff BID (LU 391)
  • Olodaterol 2.5 mcg/puff: 2 puffs OD
  • Oxeze Turbuhaler 6 mcg inhaled BID (or 12 mcg inhaled BID)
  • Foradil 12 mcg inhaled BID

Inhaler examples — LAMA

  • Spiriva Respimat 2.5 mcg/puff: 2 puffs OD
  • Aclidinium 400 mcg BID
  • Umeclidinium 62.5 mcg: 1 puff OD

Combination LAMA + LABA examples

  • Aclidinium 400 mcg + Formoterol 12 mcg: 1 puff BID (LU 459)
  • Olodaterol 2.5 mcg + Tiotropium 2.5 mcg: 2 puffs once daily
  • Umeclidinium 62.5 mcg + Vilanterol 25 mcg: 1 puff OD (LU 459)

Combination LABA/LAMA/ICS examples (triple therapy)

  • Trelegy Ellipta (fluticasone 100 mcg / umeclidinium 62.5 mcg / vilanterol 25 mcg): 1 puff OD (LU 567)
  • Breztri Aerosphere (budesonide 160 mcg / glycopyrrolate 9 mcg / formoterol 4.8 mcg): 2 puffs BID (LU 638)

If persistent symptom burden despite LAMA + LABA

Add inhaled steroid by switching to a LAMA/LABA/ICS combination puffer. Also consider referral to pulmonary rehabilitation (can improve symptoms considerably).

Escalation for frequent AECOPD despite triple therapy

If FEV1 <80% and the patient is taking LAMA/LABA/ICS and has either:

  • 2 AECOPD within the last year, or
  • 1 AECOPD that sent them to the ER or resulted in hospital admission

Add: azithromycin 250 mg OD (consider risk of hearing impairment and QT prolongation with arrhythmias).
