Smoking cessation (STOP programming)

STOP Programming is available at Kingston Community Health Centres sites and accepts self-referrals.

Patients can call the following numbers for programming at each site:

  • Barrack Site: (613) 549-1440
  • Weller Site: (613) 542-2949
  • Midtown Kingston Health Home site: (613) 542-6793

More information: https://kchc.ca/programs/smoking-cessation-2/
