About this tool

Created by

A clinician fills in this plan with the patient. It works best alongside self-management education and follow-up, not instead of them.

Default wording follows Canadian Thoracic Society and GOLD 2026 guidance, in plain language aimed at about a grade 6 reading level. Breathing skills follow those taught in Canadian pulmonary rehabilitation. The yellow-zone step approach was informed by Lung Foundation Australia's My COPD Action Plan; this tool is not endorsed by Lung Foundation Australia.

English is the source text. Other languages are draft translations: have a qualified medical translator check them before you give them to patients.
